FELIXFORGE ACADEMY UPDATE
=================

This update includes the FelixForge Academy rebrand, a redesigned light theme, styled Advanced Courses pages, six additional premium course tracks, and server-side Premium access checks.

IMPORTANT DATABASE NOTE
- Back up codecraft.db before updating.
- Keep your existing .env file and codecraft.db file.
- Do not replace your database with a blank one.
- The app adds the Course.access_level column automatically if it is missing; existing courses default to free.
- Existing accounts, progress, bookmarks, notes and payment records are retained when you keep the existing database.

PREMIUM ACCESS
- Advanced lesson content is available only when a user has a verified monthly or annual payment with status=paid, a valid starts_at, and an ends_at timestamp in the future.
- The configured administrator can access all advanced lessons without a Premium payment.
- Free users can see course descriptions and the Premium prompt, but lesson pages, practice, quizzes, notes, bookmarks and completion actions are protected on the server.
- Paystack verification remains server-side. Do not place the Paystack secret key in templates or browser JavaScript.

BRANDING
- Website name: FelixForge Academy
- Main motto: From Zero to Developer
- Supporting line: Learn coding at your own pace.
- Retained phrases: Turn curiosity into real-world code; Your next chapter starts here.

After copying the updated files, restart the Flask application. If deploying publicly, retain the same SECRET_KEY and DATABASE_URL and use HTTPS.
