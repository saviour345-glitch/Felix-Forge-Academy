How to get the most from practice
  1. Read the requirements and write down the expected behaviour.
  2. Plan the solution before coding.
  3. Build a small working version, then improve it.
  4. Test normal inputs, boundary values and invalid inputs.
  5. Explain your decisions in a short README.
